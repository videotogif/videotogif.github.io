Privacy Policy

Video to GIF converter puts your privacy first

Summary

Video to GIF converter runs 100% client-side. Video files are never sent to a server, and no user-identifying information (such as sign-up or login details) is collected.

🚫

No Personal Information Collected

No personal information, such as your name or email, is requested or stored in order to use the service.

💾

Local Processing

Uploaded videos are processed only in your browser's memory, and generated GIFs are saved only on your device.

🔒

Secure Connection

Pages are served over HTTPS, and no personal information is sent over the network.

📝

No Logs or History

Logs such as conversion history and file metadata are not collected or retained.

1) Information Collected

The operator does not request or store personal information such as your name or email.

  • Video files are read and processed only in your browser and are never sent to a server.
  • Generated GIF files are saved only on your device.
  • There is no sign-up or login, and no information is requested to identify users.
  • To improve the site, Google Analytics uses cookies to automatically collect visit statistics (pages visited, time spent, referral sources, browser and device type, and approximate country/city). This information is viewed only in aggregate statistical form and does not include the contents of your videos or GIF files.

2) Cookies and Local Storage

To provide a better experience, some preferences (e.g., dark mode, frame interval, quality, FPS) may be saved in your browser's local storage. This information does not identify you, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

The operator does not use cookies directly, but Google AdSense and Google Analytics, described in section 4 below, may use cookies to serve ads and measure visit statistics.

3) How Data Is Processed

  • ✅ Video upload: The file is read only in your browser.
  • ✅ Frame extraction: Processed locally with the Canvas API.
  • ✅ GIF conversion: Encoded inside your browser with WebAssembly.
  • ✅ Download: The result is saved only on your device.

4) Third-Party Services and Advertising

This site displays Google AdSense ads to keep the service free. Ads do not access the videos or GIF files you are converting.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • You can learn how Google uses data in Google's partner sites policy.

Visit statistics are measured with Google Analytics. If you don't want Google Analytics to collect your data, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or block cookies in your browser.

The site is also hosted on GitHub Pages, and blog comments use giscus, which is based on GitHub Discussions. Each of these services has its own privacy policy, so please review those policies as well.

5) Your Rights

  • ✅ You can delete local storage and cookie data in your browser settings.
  • ✅ Closing the page immediately stops any conversion in progress.

6) Children's Privacy

This service does not collect personal information from children under the age of 14. (In fact, it does not collect any personal information at all.)

7) Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in response to changes in the law or the service. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
