How to trim a GIF

  1. Upload your GIF

    Drag a GIF file onto the box above or click to choose one. Nothing is uploaded; the file is processed inside your browser.

  2. Pick a mode and a range

    "Keep selection" keeps only the part between the handles; "Cut out middle" removes that part and joins what comes before and after. Drag the green handles at both ends of the timeline to set the range. The frame at the handle shows up above while you drag. On a phone, just drag with your finger.

  3. Trim and save

    Click the trim button below the timeline to create the new GIF, then save it with Download GIF.

Good to know

  • The original duration of every frame is kept, so the trimmed GIF plays at the same speed as the original.
  • The selected part is re-encoded, so the file size isn't exactly proportional to the original.
  • Reading GIF frames relies on a browser feature (ImageDecoder). It works best in the latest Chrome or Edge.
  • Don't have a GIF yet? Make one with the video to GIF converter first, then trim it here. Cutting the length is the most reliable way to shrink a GIF.
